Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Governing law: England and Wales

This Privacy Policy explains how Brink Browser ("Brink", "we", "us") handles your data. We believe privacy should be the default, not a feature. Please read this carefully.

1. Who We Are

Brink Browser is developed and operated by Josh Bradley, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app.

2. The Short Version

Brink uses Firebase — Google's app development platform — to collect anonymous crash reports, performance diagnostics and usage analytics. This helps us fix bugs and improve the app. We never collect your browsing content, research notes, saved pages or recordings. Everything you create in Brink stays on your device.

3. Data We Collect

Brink uses Firebase (provided by Google LLC) for crash reporting, performance monitoring, push notifications and analytics. The data collected through Firebase is used solely to improve app stability, performance and your experience. The following data types are collected:

Category Data Type Purpose Linked to You?
Diagnostics Crash Data Analytics, App Functionality Not linked
Diagnostics Performance Data Analytics, App Functionality Not linked
Diagnostics Other Diagnostic Data Analytics, App Functionality Not linked
Identifiers Device ID Analytics, App Functionality Not linked
Usage Data Product Interaction Analytics, App Functionality Not linked
Location Coarse Location Analytics, App Functionality Not linked

None of this data includes your browsing history, search queries, research folder contents, notes, recordings or any other content you create inside Brink. All collected data is anonymised and not linked to your identity.

For full details on how Firebase handles data, see Google's Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

4. What We Do Not Collect

Brink never collects, stores or transmits the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Search queries entered in the URL bar
  • Research folder contents, saved pages or bookmarks
  • Quick notes or annotations
  • Lecture recordings or audio files
  • Precise location data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)

All content you create within the app — your folders, notes, saved research, recordings — is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

5. Microphone Access

Brink requests access to your device microphone solely to support the Lecture Capture feature, which allows you to record audio directly within the app. This permission is only active while you are actively recording.

Microphone recordings are stored entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted or shared with us or any third party. You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device Settings.

6. Push Notifications

Brink uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications. If you grant notification permission, a Firebase-generated push token is used solely to route notifications to your device. This token is not linked to your personal identity and is not used for any other purpose.

7. Data Apple May Collect

Brink is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain data as part of the App Store experience, including purchase records and download information, in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not control or have access to this data.

8. Contact Form (Website)

If you use the contact form on our website, the information you submit (name, email address and message) is used solely to respond to your enquiry. This data is not stored beyond the purpose of your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.

9. Children's Privacy

Brink is designed for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK-based product, we are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Your rights include:

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app.

11. Data Security

Data collected via Firebase is stored on Google's infrastructure and governed by Google's security practices. Content you create within the app is stored locally on your device, protected by your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. We recommend keeping your device and iOS up to date.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes we will notify users via an in-app notice and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

13. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app or via the contact form.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Governing law: England and Wales

This Privacy Policy explains how Brink Browser ("Brink", "we", "us") handles your data. We believe privacy should be the default, not a feature. Please read this carefully.

1. Who We Are

Brink Browser is developed and operated by Josh Bradley, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app.

2. The Short Version

Right now, Brink does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app — your browsing, your research folders, your notes, your recordings — stays entirely on your device. We cannot see it, access it or share it.

This policy also discloses what data may be collected in future versions of the app, so you know what to expect before it happens. We will update this policy and notify users before any new data collection begins.

3. Data We Currently Collect

In the current version of Brink: none.

The app does not collect, store or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Search queries
  • Research folder contents, notes or saved pages
  • Lecture recordings
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage statistics or analytics

All data created within the app is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard storage APIs and is not transmitted anywhere.

4. Data Apple May Collect

Brink is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain data as part of the App Store experience, including purchase records and download information, in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not control or have access to this data.

5. Future Data Collection — What May Change

We want to be transparent about features we are considering for future versions. The following types of data collection may be introduced in a future update. This policy will be updated and you will be informed before any of these are introduced:

  • Crash reporting — we may integrate a service such as Firebase Crashlytics to automatically capture crash logs and stack traces. This would help us identify and fix bugs. Crash reports typically include device model, OS version, app version and the code location of the crash, but not personal data or browsing content.
  • Analytics — we may introduce anonymised usage analytics to understand which features are used and how the app performs. This would never include browsing content, research data, notes or recordings.
  • Approximate location from IP address — if analytics are introduced, we may derive an approximate country or region from your IP address for the sole purpose of understanding our user base geographically. This would be approximate only and not precise location data.

None of the above are active in the current version. We will always tell you before they are.

6. Contact Form (Website)

If you use the contact form on our website, the information you submit (name, email address and message) is used solely to respond to your enquiry. This data is not stored beyond the purpose of your enquiry and is not shared with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Brink is designed for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK-based product, we are committed to complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Since we currently collect no personal data, most of these rights are not currently applicable — but they will apply if data collection is introduced in future. Your rights include:

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal data
  • The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app.

9. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is primarily governed by your device's own security — including your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. We recommend keeping your device and iOS up to date.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes — particularly any that introduce data collection — we will notify users via an in-app notice and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@brinkbrowser.app or via the contact form.

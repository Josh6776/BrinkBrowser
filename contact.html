What’s new in Brink

Brink’s research workspace just got a major upgrade.

  • Organise your research into separate projects
  • Keep tabs, folders and saved research organised by project
  • Swipe between research projects from the sidebar
  • Improved citations, including Harvard, IEEE and Chicago styles
  • Improved user experience in the sidebar
  • Better folder organisation with nested folders
  • Press and hold New Tab to create a private one
  • Improved PDFs, images, notes and lecture captures
  • Smarter Brink Spotlight search across your research and tabs
  • Improved browser back and forward navigation
  • UI improvements and bug fixes throughout Brink

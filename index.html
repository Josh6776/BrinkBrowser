Designed for iPad

Built for the screen
you already study on

Brink is built from the ground up for iPad. The sidebar sits naturally alongside your browsing, your research folders are always one tap away, and the whole interface scales beautifully from iPad mini to iPad Pro.

Every layout decision was made with a student at a desk in mind. Brink feels like it belongs on iPad because it was designed there first.

Move saved pages between folders with a long press. Generate Harvard, APA or MLA citations from anything you've saved, with one tap, your ready to paste. Keep lecture recordings, notes and links together in the same folder so nothing gets lost.

About the developer

Built out of
frustration

Brink started as a personal project. A student frustrated with losing research tabs, copying citations by hand, and switching between four different apps just to get through a study session.

The idea was simple: what if your browser was actually designed for studying? Brink is that browser. It's an independent app, made by one person, with the goal of making deep work a little easier.

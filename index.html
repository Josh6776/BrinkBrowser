    What’s coming in Brink 3.0

    Brink 3.0 is still in testing and has not been released yet. When it arrives it will be free to download on iPad, with one-tap Project Bibliographies, CrossRef DOI lookup, and Apple Pencil PDF markup.

    • Free to try, with a one-time Brink Pro unlock Brink will be free to try on iPad. A one-time £4.99 Brink Pro unlock will add unlimited projects, citations, and bibliography exports, with no subscriptions. Previous paid buyers will get Brink Pro at no extra cost.
    • One-Tap Project Bibliography Export Compile an entire research project or folder into an alphabetised Harvard, APA 7, MLA 9, or Chicago Reference List, or export numbered IEEE, BibTeX (.bib), or RIS (.ris) files.
